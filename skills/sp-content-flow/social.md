# Social notes (Reddit, X, Discord, LinkedIn)

## r/NFLSurvivor rules to check on every draft
- **Rule 3 (no spam or ads) and Rule 5 (say if you're connected to what you share).** A linked post whose body carries survivorpulse.com links reads as a product link by proxy, even from a disclosed mod. Put a short disclosure at the link itself: "(it mentions SurvivorPulse, which I build)".
- **Link:** last, one only, a plain optional offer. Wording must match what the linked post truly is. A results recap is not "how I thought about Week 7".
- A founder-requested link a reviewer wants cut is a founder decision: offer A (link) and B (no link) with a recommendation.

## Fact traps
- The rankings page rounds Field Exposure to ONE decimal. A founder-quoted 0.03% cannot be confirmed (page shows 0.0%); say so, do not assert it. Win probability (e.g. 29%) can be confirmed.
- Do not claim a data source (e.g. "Yahoo") the page does not state.

## Reply craft
- Frame a risky pick as a tradeoff, not an endorsement, especially for beginners.
- Credit others in the thread where they are right.
- Public reply: no sign-off. DM: sign-off.
