# Notes for non-social types

## Email (reviewer: Cara)
- Give Cara the whole thread and send context: recipient, relationship, what was asked, deadline.
- She checks reply-vs-reply-all, tone fit for the recipient, missing asks, and whether it needs sending at all.
- Sky drafts the body; email gets a short sign-off. The founder sends. Never use a send or draft-create tool without his say-so.

## Articles and white papers (reviewers: Vlad, plus Marie Curie and Paige as needed)
- Vlad checks every claim and number against the real data, and says which are confirmed, derived, or unconfirmed.
- Research-heavy (backtests, strategy claims): also `boa-marie-curie` for analytical credibility. Claims about accuracy or edge need a named source in the repo or docs.
- Anything for the public site or search: also `paige-the-page` for SEO, structure, and conversion fit.
- Founder publishes, or Arlo schedules after approval.

## Help guides (reviewer: Vlad; loader: Oscar)
- Vlad checks each step against the live product (use `sp-live-verify`). A step he cannot reproduce is a defect in the guide.
- After founder approval, `oscar-the-operator` may load the text into Admin Hub as a DRAFT only. He never publishes; the founder does.

## Distribution (Arlo)
- `arlo-the-amplifier` publishes or schedules only after the founder has approved the exact final text. Re-confirm if the text changed after approval.
