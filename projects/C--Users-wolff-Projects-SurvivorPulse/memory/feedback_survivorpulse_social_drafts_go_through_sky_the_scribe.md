All SurvivorPulse social content flows through three steps: Sky drafts, Meg reviews replies, the founder approves.

- **Sky-the-Scribe drafts everything.** Posts, replies, reposts, comments, quote-posts, DMs, on Reddit, X, Discord and LinkedIn. The `authentic-tone-of-voice` skill is loaded. The main session does not draft these inline.
- **Meg-the-Megaphone reviews Sky's reply drafts** in the context of the whole thread (what others said, what the OP already got, who the other commenters are), and sends change recommendations back to Sky. Meg recommends; she doesn't rewrite.
- **The founder approves all outgoing content** before it posts. For now this is the final gate; Meg's review doesn't replace it.

**Why:** On 2026-10-28 the founder asked whether Sky was involved in the r/NFLSurvivor reply drafts to u/DingwadtheDunce. She wasn't; the main session drafted them inline. The founder then set this flow: Sky owns drafting, Meg adds thread-level review, the founder stays the approver.

**How to apply:**
- Brief Sky with the real facts (win probability, field exposure, thread context, what was already said). Sky has no live data, so don't let her fill placeholders.
- Send Sky's reply draft to Meg with the full thread, get her recommendations, then have Sky revise. Present the final draft to the founder, with Meg's notes.
- The main session still fact-checks every number before the founder sees the draft.
- Meg's review applies to replies. Original posts (Weekly Rankings, research series) go Sky, then founder, unless the founder says to add Meg.
- **Scope widened 2026-10-29 to all written content**, same shape each time (Sky drafts, a reviewer sends recommendations, main session fact-checks and triages, founder approves; agents never send or publish). Reviewers: Meg for social replies, Cara for emails, Vlad for articles/white papers/help guides (plus Stan and Marie Curie for research-heavy, Paige for public/SEO), Oscar loads approved help guides as DRAFT only, Arlo distributes only after approval.
- The runbook, with the lessons from the DingwadtheDunce reply (blind links, 1-decimal Field Exposure, Rule 3/5 disclosure, triage), is the user-level skill `sp-content-flow`. Invoke it; don't restate it here.
