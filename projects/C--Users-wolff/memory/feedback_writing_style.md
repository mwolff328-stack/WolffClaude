Never use em-dashes in any content written for or as the user. Use periods or line breaks instead.

**Why:** User explicitly said content sounds AI-generated when em-dashes are used. They are a 50-year-old male who wants writing to sound like a real person talking, not an analyst summarizing.

**How to apply:** In all Reddit comments, social content, or any copy written in the user's voice — zero em-dashes, full stop. Short sentences. Conversational vocabulary. Sounds like someone who watched the games, not read the recap.

**He signs public-facing content as "Mike," not "Michael"** (confirmed 2026-08-17, editing a mod message and a Reddit/Discord post). Drafts that introduce him as Michael get corrected. Use Mike anywhere he's speaking as himself in public. Also prefers "discuss" over combative framing like "argue about it" when describing how he'll engage in comments.
