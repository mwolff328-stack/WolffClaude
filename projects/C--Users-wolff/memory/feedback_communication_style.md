Keep responses plain and concise. Michael finds technical jargon hard to follow and wants to make decisions quickly.

**Why:** Responses were too technical and complicated, slowing down consumption and decision-making.

**How to apply:**
- Use everyday words instead of technical terms. If a technical term is necessary, explain it in one plain phrase.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- State what you did, what you found, or what decision is needed — in that order.
- No long reasoning chains in the response. Think it through internally, then surface just the conclusion and the "so what."
- When a decision is needed, say what the options are and give a clear recommendation in plain terms.
